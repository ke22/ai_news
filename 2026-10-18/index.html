Coupled with Vercel’s data indicating unprecedented adoption of lightweight decision models alongside open-source growth, the industry is moving away from monolithic cloud endpoints toward a distributed, hybrid infrastructure where local edge processing and specialized multimodal reasoning capabilities work in tandem.
