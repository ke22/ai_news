Meanwhile, geopolitical friction between the US and China is intensifying around open-weight models and knowledge transfer. Accusations of unauthorized model distillation—where Chinese developers train lighter models using outputs from US systems—have prompted US sanction threats, while Beijing defends distillation as standard global practice. These compounding tensions demonstrate that AI’s growth trajectory is no longer purely driven by raw compute and algorithmic breakthroughs, but by international regulatory compliance, capital sustainability, and sovereign technology control.
